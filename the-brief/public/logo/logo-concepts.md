# The Brief - Logo Concepts

## Design Requirements
- Clean, modern, trustworthy aesthetic
- NZ political theme (fern, parliament, voting elements)
- Works in dark/light modes
- Simple enough for favicon (16x16, 32x32)
- Professional political journalism branding

## Concept 1: Silver Fern + Document
**Visual:** Stylized silver fern leaf integrated with a folded document/brief corner
- Icon: Fern fronds forming the corner of a document
- Typography: Clean sans-serif "THE BRIEF" 
- Color: Monochrome (works on any background)
- Favicon: Just the fern-document icon

## Concept 2: Parliament Dome Silhouette
**Visual:** Simplified NZ Parliament Beehive outline with modern line work
- Icon: Geometric beehive/dome shape with horizontal lines (representing legislative text)
- Typography: Bold condensed "THE BRIEF"
- Color: Dark/light mode variants (charcoal/white)
- Favicon: Simplified dome icon

## Concept 3: Voting Tick + Speech Bubble
**Visual:** Check mark combined with conversation bubble
- Icon: Tick mark forming the tail of a speech bubble
- Typography: Medium weight "The Brief" with subtitle "NZ Politics"
- Color: Single color with transparency for overlay use
- Favicon: Just the tick-bubble mark

## Concept 4: Koru + Breaking News Banner
**Visual:** Traditional Koru spiral with banner ribbon
- Icon: Unfurling koru (new beginning) with news banner
- Typography: Serif "THE BRIEF" for editorial authority
- Color: Two-tone (fern green + neutral)
- Favicon: Koru spiral only

## Concept 5: Minimalist TB Monogram
**Visual:** Interlocking T+B letterforms with fern frond detail
- Icon: TB monogram with subtle fern texture/pattern
- Typography: "THE BRIEF" in architectural font (parliament building reference)
- Color: Monochrome with optional accent
- Favicon: TB monogram perfectly suited for small sizes

## Implementation Notes
- Primary logo: Full lockup with icon + text
- Secondary: Icon only (square format for social media)
- Favicon: 16x16, 32x32, 48x48 variants
- SVG master files for scalability
- PNG exports: transparent background, multiple sizes
