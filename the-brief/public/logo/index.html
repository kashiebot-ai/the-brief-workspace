The Brief - Logo Variations

1. Fern Document
Clean document outline with silver fern overlay. Combines briefing paper concept with NZ identity. Works well at small sizes.
2. Parliament Dome
Simplified parliamentary building with dome and columns. Abstract document fold beneath. Strong political connection.
3. Ballot Checkmark Fern
Voting checkbox with checkmark transforming into fern fronds. Merges democratic process with NZ symbolism. Unique and memorable.
4. Minimalist "B"
Geometric letterform for "Brief" with subtle document fold lines. Small fern accent. Most abstract, works excellently as favicon.
5. NZ Abstract Circle
Circular frame with unfurling fern (koru style) and subtle NZ map. Document corner accent. Most distinctly Kiwi design.
